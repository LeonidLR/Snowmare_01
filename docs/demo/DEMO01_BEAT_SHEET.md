# DEMO 01: Beat Sheet and Graybox Spec: «Frozen Lake» -> «Auto-Repair Garage» — REV 5

> **User flow 2026-10-13 (rev 5, AUTHORITATIVE; supersedes rev 4 and the 2026-10-11 decision "no enemies on the lake before the attack").** The designer's new Demo 01 flow, in his order: (1) the level opens with **radio / HQ only** - no lessons, no actions while HQ talks; **the inventory is locked, sprint is locked**; (2) the staged camera lesson; (3) walk to beacon 1 (walking only); (4) a crevasse with a **shaky bridge: the squad crosses ONE AT A TIME** = select an operative + Solo; (5) after the bridge the squad walk-and-talk (the banter that used to open A0; the HQ briefing stays at the start); (6) the warming lesson: **heat pack** (every operative starts with 1, `squad_loadout.heat_packs_count: 1`), **the inventory unlocks here**, the gust safety stays; (7) "running warms you up" -> **sprint unlocks here** (not at beacon 1); (8) the fishermen's hut: supplies / loot, more heat packs (existing hut lesson, resupply line + objective); (9) move on; cold again, need heat again; they find a barrel -> **light the barrel** (existing A2 lesson); (10) approach the complex **cautiously** (sprint not locked): mines and tripwires, look for cover, **crawl prone under a low fence**; stances and vaulting are taught here; (11) they spot enemies: **patrolling frozen (zombie-type) + a pair of hounds**; the player lays an ambush with **tripwires**; a **vision-cone toggle (Commandos style, hotkey + menu entry)**; a small fight; (12) inside the base: look around, another supply spot, **one crate is booby-trapped -> disarm**; (13) Commander: "be ready for a flank attack, prepare the place": **move barricades, mine (booby-trap) barricades, move barrels (ambush or warmth), take dominant / high positions (colder up there)**; (14) the **canister (fuel) quest**; (15) the **horde** lessons: first the **tactical pause**, second **automatic mode (Commander Mode / AUTO)**, third: after the first attack the horde wrecks the generator -> **repair it with the Engineer and make it his guard point**.

> **Defaults set by the orchestrator 2026-10-13 (implemented; the user may change any of them):**
> - **Shaky bridge:** a second operative is NOT allowed on while one is crossing: the bridge creaks, the second stops at the edge with a feed line ("The bridge won't hold two!"), no damage, no falling. Formation followers wait at the bridge end until it is free. A generic placeable C++ actor (`ABridgeCrossingVolume`, Capacity 1 by default, data-driven per instance), usable on any map.
> - **Low fence:** MANUAL prone - standing / crouched operatives cannot pass (the move stops at the fence with the hint "Too low - go prone"), prone ones crawl under. Generic placeable C++ actor (`ACrawlGapActor`); pathing: non-prone paths route around or end at the fence (crawl-only navmesh area closed to `UNavFilter_NoCrawl`), prone paths may go under; enemies never path under.
> - **Box select** is taught after the bridge (regroup everyone with a box and go on). **Guard** is taught in step 15 (the Engineer guarding the repaired generator), not in A1 any more.
> - **Inventory lock:** a tutorial lock target for the inventory drawer (`Inventory.Open`, key I / action bar) and item use (`Item.*`: U and H/J/K/L, item drags), unlocked by the heat-pack lesson card.
> - **HQ intro:** the radio step also locks `Order.Move` while it plays (a click gets one feed line "Hold on - HQ is talking."); the first camera card unlocks moves.
> - **Precredit** (user addition 2026-10-13): a command lesson the player already performed before its card is due (panned / rotated / zoomed, MMB-dragged, selected a member, used stance keys, ... since the level started, even during the HQ intro) completes without showing the card (one feed tick "already done"); partial uses seed the card's progress; opt-out per step `"precredit": false` (location- / target-bound lessons: regroup, sprint, loot open, tripwire); event / volume completions are never precredited.
> - **Camera lesson** (user addition 2026-10-13): a separate staged step for the **middle-mouse drag pan** (`A0_cam_pan_drag`, `TUT_A0_CAM_PAN_DRAG`) after the WASD / edge pan and before Q/E.

> **Game designer's decisions 2026-10-19 b (second play-test of the lake):** (1) **The ambush, part 2: lure the second enemy.** Part 1 (as before): rig a tripwire on one enemy's route; that enemy walks into it and dies (objective "Wait until the patrol walks into the wire"). Part 2 (new): the other enemy is LURED - card "LURE - Whistle [5] from cover to call him to you, or throw a stone [6] to send him where you want." unlocks `Stealth.*`. **Decisions (Claude):** (i) part 1 completes on an ENEMY pulling the pin of any wire (event `TripwireTriggered.Enemy`; 240 s at most - the lure part follows either way); (ii) part 2 completes when the remaining enemy REACTS to a lure: it heard it and investigates (event `StealthLure.Heard`) - robust whatever the player does next (walking into a second wire, the squad's fire, nothing); (iii) then a short card suggests AGGRESSIVE ("the squad opens fire the moment he is in sight - or Ctrl+click him"), done on Posture.Aggressive / a targeted shot / the fight starting; the fight itself stays Phase 2; (iv) **checked on the lake (dev probe, also on a copy of the user's map with his frostbitten + hound):** the routes pass 12-16 m from the overlook - a whistle from the overlook is never heard (12 m, the low cover muffles it), a stone thrown crouched from behind `Overlook_LowCover` lures the frostbitten without detection; the card adds "from here a stone near his path works best". **For the user (map, optional):** to let the whistle work from the overlook, bring the near leg of a patrol route within ~8-10 m of `Overlook_LowCover` (or put a low cover piece ~8 m from a route, e.g. in front of the containers) - Claude did not edit the map. (2) **Postures - Aggressive must really fire, and a posture lesson.** Semantics: **Passive** = never open fire on their own (only direct orders); **Defensive** = return fire / engage only enemies that attack or have detected the squad; **Aggressive** = open fire on any enemy in sight - in exploration too, through the normal fight start (the bug: on the lake, a «button» level with placed patrols, the Aggressive squad never fired: the opening fire asked for an ambush level only). Lesson: right after the trap disarm lesson the Commander says "Hold your fire - shoot only on my command." and the card "POSTURE - [,] PASSIVE: never fire on their own · [.] DEFENSIVE: return fire · [/] AGGRESSIVE: shoot any enemy in sight. Alt + key = the whole squad. Go PASSIVE now ..." unlocks `Posture.*` and completes on Posture.Passive. **Decisions (Claude):** (i) the lesson follows the WIRE card (`A3_wire`, the last trap before the overlook; disarmed, prone, timed out or skipped) once the cover field was reached (`requires A3_cover`), beat-less so the overlook 13 m on never drops it; a wire never spotted -> the same radio + card at the overlook (A4 start + 3 s), each pair cancels the other; (ii) the squad enters the complex (A3 start) DEFENSIVE (step action `set_posture`): with `Posture.*` still locked an Aggressive squad would start the A4 fight on its own the moment a patrol came into view; (iii) Defensive per target: an enemy hunting the squad is engaged; once attacked, any enemy for that fight; a patrol that has not spotted the squad is never fired at; (iv) the reminder to switch to AGGRESSIVE comes with the ambush (A4). (3) **Symmetric sight:** an operative sees an enemy only with a clear line of sight from HIS eye height for his stance (prone 25 / crouched 95 / standing 160 cm - the Sprint 08 heights) to the enemy's body, the same trace the enemies use - now while exploring too (before, the squad's sight ran only in a fight: in the tutorial every enemy stayed revealed). **Decisions (Claude):** an enemy no operative sees is hidden (no body, plate, cone or marker), not targetable (no fire, Ctrl+click "Can't see it from here."), unseen on the minimap; within 12 m a faint "heard" silhouette follows it; once lost a last-known silhouette stays and, exploring, fades after 12 s together with the minimap's last-known icon (in a fight it stays for blind fire). Card at the overlook (A4, the first time someone is prone while an enemy is out of sight): "OUT OF SIGHT - Prone behind cover they can't see you - but you can't see them either. Peek or rise to look." (A3 has no enemies, so the card lives in A4.)

> **Game designer's decisions 2026-10-19 (play-test of the lake demo):** (1) **Crate: the animation FIRST, then the loot.** The operative walks up, faces the crate, plays the chest-open clip; the loot window opens only at its "lid open" moment and nothing can be taken before; Take / Take All: he plays the chest-search clip and the items move into his kit at its "grab" moment, the window shows a short "Searching the crate..." meanwhile (buttons off). **Decisions (Claude):** (i) the moments are data per clip (`EffectFraction` in the ABP class defaults: open 0.9 = 2.7 s of 3.0 - the lid is up at the end of the clip; search 0.5 = 1.6 s; pickup 0.5); (ii) every take is its own search (the clip restarts; a second take while searching is refused); (iii) a collector who walks off / is knocked down before the grab takes nothing; closing the window does not cancel a running search; (iv) **tactical pause:** the opening / the search waits and plays when the pause ends (time stands still in the pause; the old rule opened the window at once without a clip). (2) **Mine / tripwire disarm: he bends down FIRST, then the trap disappears.** Success -> the pickup clip (standing: full body from the low pose; crouched: upper body; prone: none - at once); the mine / the wire disappears at the clip's grab moment, then the "disarmed" line and the kit / grenades. **Decisions (Claude):** (i) until the grab the trap is harmless (cannot go off, cannot be tripped, no menu) - the operative is safe; (ii) a cut clip (knockdown, an order) still removes the trap at the grab time. (3) **The MEDKIT lesson when an operative gets hurt.** The first time an operative drops below 70 % health (a mine / wire blast, frostbite, a lesson down at 1 HP), a card: "MEDKIT - X is wounded. It completes when a medkit heals THAT operative. **Decisions (Claude):** (i) the card belongs to no beat (it can come anywhere, a later beat does not drop it), shows once, times out after 120 s; (ii) the threshold is data (`squad_injury_max` >= 30 in the steps); (iii) it unlocks Item.Use / Inventory.Open / Item.Give (the progressive locks never block it); (iv) taught input = what works today: H uses the SELECTED operative's own medkit; a mate's medkit must be handed over first (drag onto him), then H - no "use on a mate" key was added; (v) every operative starts with one medkit (the kit default; the hut crate holds none).

## 0. One-paragraph pitch

The game starts from the CommonUI menu with NEW GAME. Three operatives (Commander, Engineer, Medic-Sapper Sokolova) set off across a frozen lake
in a rising wind, **talking as they walk**. The lake teaches the body: move, split up, regroup, crouch, go prone. Then it teaches the cold:
barrel and heat pack. Then the buildings: scout inside, loot crates, learn that the **PMC left traps in the worst places**, and
that **numb fingers cannot disarm a mine**. Stage two (rev 5): the squad approaches the PMC complex **cautiously** (stances, a crawl under a low fence, cover, mines and wires), lays a **tripwire ambush for a patrol** (two frozen, two hounds; vision cones on), loots the base (one crate is wired), **prepares the defence** (barricades, mines, barrels, the roof) and finds the **fuel canister** for the generator. The radio mast's power-up wakes the lake. The station-yard
fight is taught in **three forms** (real time -> tactical pause -> turn-based). It brings a **flank breach** through the boathouse
and a **freezing survivor (Susanin)** to rescue. The squad then takes the road to a **closed auto-repair garage**: patrols, tripwires,
mines, an ambush. If that fight runs past **4 minutes**, a **second horde** arrives, so the player learns to finish fast from the **mezzanine**.
Total ~45 min first-time (cut list to ~35 min in §2.4).

### 2.2 Defusal under cold (the core "traps + cold" lesson, existing formula)

`chance = role base + stance (stand -15 / crouch +10 / prone +25) + luck x 0.5 - cold (>25: 15, >50: 30, >75: 50) - 25 per failed attempt`.
"Dangerous" below 45 % gives a warning first; a failure detonates 40-80 %.

| Operative (base, luck) | crouched, cold <= 25 | 26-50 | 51-75 | > 75 | prone, cold <= 25 | prone, 51-75 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Medic-Sapper (60, 35) | **88 %** | 73 % | 58 % | **38 % (danger)** | 99 % | 73 % |
| Engineer (45, 30) | 70 % | 55 % | 40 % (danger) | 20 % | 85 % | 55 % |
| Commander (35, 25) | 58 % | 43 % (danger) | 28 % | 8 % | 73 % | 43 % |

The action menu already prints "Operator: X (Stance) | Success chance ~N %", and the warning line says "my fingers are numb" when cold.
Design every trap lesson so that the **cold Medic-Sapper, crouched, reads ~38-58 %**, warms up, and comes back to **88-99 %**.

## 5. Combat section A: three forms, flank breach, rescue

### 5.1 How the existing systems behave (facts)
- **Trigger:** using the console (`GateTerminal`) calls `UQuestSubsystem::StartPreCombatCutscene` -> `UGameFlowSubsystem::TriggerCombatZone`. With the default **sudden start** the wave spawns at once with no countdown.
- **Sudden start timeline** (`HordeIntroRules`): wave spawns -> **real time** while the mass forms (min delay, max wait) -> **free tactical pause** (no charge, no timer) + slow-motion flyover over every new enemy type + "HORDE INCOMING" -> Space tap resumes real time; **hold Space 1.5 s = turn-based**.
  So every sudden wave already shows real time -> pause -> (turn-based on hold), in that order.
- **Between waves:** `WaveCleared` stops time and shows the victory panel with "Start the next wave (N/3)". With `sudden_start` true the next wave has no rest countdown. Each panel is a natural lesson break.
- **Flank breach** (`AEnemySpawnPoint.bIsDynamic`, `UWaveSubsystem::CheckDynamicFlankSpawners` / `TriggerBreach`, Godot `_check_dynamic_flank_spawners`): in the mission's breach wave the first dynamic point that passes `ActivationChance` (default 0.7) breaks through **4-7 s after the wave starts**. `EnemyCount` (default 4) of `BreachEnemyType` spawn round the point (types other than Spitter / Brute / Cutter spawn hounds, Godot parity). HQ feed line "FLANK BREACH!". After 1 s the camera shows the point for 1.8 s with "Damn it, where did they come from?", then returns. **During turn-based combat the breach is deferred until TB ends** (`RunOrDeferRandomEvent`). One per mission. **Dormant today:** no map has a dynamic point; the wave is random in [1,2,3].
  A second, TB-only kind of breakthrough exists too: **exposed zones** (`ExposedZones`). A squad that stays exposed in one quadrant for 3 TB turns gets a warning (1), danger (2), then a breach of 1-2 non-elite enemies, once per fight.
- **Rescue** (`URecruitSubsystem`, Godot `recruit_susanin.gd`): in the mission's rescue wave, 5-8 s after it starts (deferred past TB), **Ivan Susanin** spawns from the operative BP at the `TargetPoint` tagged `SusaninSpawn`. He is freezing (cold 85), with a narrative pause, a camera focus and a blocking distress dialogue. **"Help" = any living operative within 2.8 m of him, or Susanin inside any active heat source** (a lit barrel within 4 m). Then a recruitment dialogue, and he joins as **member 4** (warm, armed). His death does not fail the mission. Today the wave is random and must differ from the breach wave.
- **Generator under attack** (`AInteractableActor` Generator): small enemies weigh the generator at 0.4 x the squad's distance (so they rush it), turrets at 0.5 x. At 0 HP: breakdown, heat off, **all turrets unpowered**, the «[EMERGENCY]» menu appears, and (new gate rule) **door opening pauses**. Repair = select the Engineer, click the generator -> Repair (**2.5 s**; others 5 s). Turret repair: Engineer 2 s, others 4 s. **Barricades cannot be repaired** (gap G18). Repair inside a running fight: the use-order path exists for barrel lighting (`UseOrderRules`, real time = walk up and use; pause = planned). For generator / turret repair in a live fight it **must be verified** (BotRepairSmoke covers only the preparation phase): smoke `RepairInCombatSmoke` (G18).
- **Victory:** the last wave dead -> "End combat and continue exploring" -> PostCombat (deployables recovered, the squad set back at its stations) -> exploration. With the new gate rule the blast door finishes opening at this point and is the exit.
  **Defeat:** only the Commander's death (death cinematic, "MISSION FAILED"). There is no defeat for losing the generator: it only costs heat, turret power and door time.

## 8. Combat section B: patrols, traps, 4-minute horde, heights

### 8.1 Facts from code
- **Ambush start** (`ULevelEncounterSubsystem`): with `combat_start: "ambush"` (or Auto with placed enemies), the fight starts **once**, when a patrol detects the squad (sight / hearing / smell), the squad hurts or attacks an enemy, an aggressive operative opens fire, or an enemy attacks. All living enemies in the world become the wave (`AdoptLevelEnemies`). `bAmbushSingleFight` = the fight is the whole battle -> PostCombat.
- **Mid-fight horde** (`UHordeSubsystem`, `horde.json`): a clock counts **only full real-time fight seconds** (not the tactical pause, **not turn-based**, not dialogues). At **240 s** it releases **8** enemies (5 FROST_HOUND + 3 FROSTBITTEN) at a reachable point **25-45 m** from the squad centre, out of sight, clustered within 4 m. They know where the squad is and rush it, with a HUD "HORDE!" banner + arrow + radio line (warning 6 s). Once per fight (`repeats: false`). Per-level override `"horde": {...}`; console `Codex.Horde.TriggerSeconds`.
- **Heights:** see §2.1. Real time only (TB requires flat ground). Spitters prefer elevated targets (+50), Marksmen go prone on heights, the bot values elevated cover +25. **There is no on-screen feedback that the bonus is active** (gap G11).

### 8.2 Encounter flow
1. **Contact** (any trigger) -> "We've been spotted! Engage!" -> real-time fight with 8 placed enemies (BeforeCombat autosave = `CP_B3`).
2. **Ideal play (~2.5-3.5 min):** two operatives on the mezzanine fire at +25 % / +15 %, a third holds the SW stair top (T guard, crouched behind the railing). The re-rigged tripwire and the tripwire grenades take P1. The oil drums pushed to the stair foot are Ctrl+click targets. The turret covers the roller door; if it breaks, the Engineer repairs it (2 s).
3. **Clock pressure** (new, G12): at **180 s** of real-time fight a radio warning: `WT_B3_CLOCK` "Captain, that noise carries for kilometres. Finish it or we'll have company." Plus a small fight timer on the combat badge (mm:ss of the horde clock). **Card `TUT_B3_LASTCHANCE`:** "Tap Space. In the pause you can still lay a mine or a barricade within 12 m: which door is open?" (pause placement, §2.6). Done on `DeployablePlaced(bInPause)` (E).
4. **240 s** -> the horde: 8 enemies appear **out of sight, 20-40 m away** (random reachable point), so they come through **whichever door faces them**. The door traps from B2 / B3 decide how many arrive. Per-level override: `"horde": { "trigger_seconds": 240, "min_distance_m": 20, "max_distance_m": 40 }`, so they spawn in the yard, not beyond the map edge (45 m from a hall-centred squad is off the 70 x 50 yard). The heights make this survivable: one entry (stairs) per side.
5. **Victory:** all adopted + horde enemies dead -> PostCombat -> B4.
