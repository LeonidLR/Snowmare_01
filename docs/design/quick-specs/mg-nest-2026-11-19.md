# Quick Design Spec: Mounted machine-gun nest

**Type**: New Small System
**Scope**: A stationary, single-gunner machine-gun emplacement any side can man (PMC, survivors, neophytes, player operatives),
working in real time and in the turn-based grid fight. Out of scope: carrying / redeploying the gun, destroying the gun itself,
generator-powered automatic turrets (docs/design/TURRET_POWER.md), the squad play "guard the gunner's flanks" (after T-05).
**Date**: 2026-11-19 (game designer approved, quick-design session with Claude)
**Estimated Implementation**: ~5 working days
**Queue**: after smart-enemies T-05 (firing positions) - the nest is a special post; without T-05 the PMC would man it bunched.

## Overview

A machine-gun nest is a gun on a mount behind a shield and sandbags, placed at crossroads or on roofs. While a gunner sits at it,
it holds a 90-120 degree sector with dense, high-penetration fire, strong suppression and good frontal protection. Its weaknesses
are slow traverse, open flanks and rear, and audible silent windows for belt changes and overheating. Without a gunner the nest
belongs to nobody: any side may take it, including a player operative after the crew died.

Code basis: AMortarNestActor pattern (crew place, level JSON placement, placeholder visuals), ArmorRules (penetration class),
SuppressionRules (`weight.machine_gun` 0.09 already in Content/Data/Combat/suppression.json), the Gorky grid (150 cm cells, 6 AP).

## Core Rules

1. **Nest** = position, facing, sector width, starting owner side, one gunner place. Placed from the level JSON block `mg_nests`
   (same pattern as `mortar_nests`).
2. **One gunner.** Mounting 1.5 s (turn-based 2 AP), dismounting 1 s (1 AP). The mounted gunner does not move and does not use
   his own weapon.
3. **Sector.** Fire only inside the sector arc. The barrel traverses at most 45 deg/s: a target at the sector edge is reached late.
4. **Cover.** Shield + sandbags give the gunner high cover only against shots arriving from inside the sector widened by +/-15 deg.
   From the flanks, the rear and above (a roof sniper) he is exposed. Explosions near the nest hit him normally.
5. **Fire.** Bursts of 8-12 rounds, 600 rounds/min, penetration class Heavy (the class rule "weapon class >= armour class" applies
   against every armour). Each round suppresses with `weight.machine_gun`.
6. **Silent windows.** Belt of 100 rounds, change 8 s. Heat rises per round and decays over time; at full heat the gun is silent
   for 4 s. Both are audible (bolt click, belt change sound) - the player's windows to rush.
7. **Turn-based.** Available when the nest is on the grid and a gunner already sits at it when the turn-based fight starts. A free
   nest can be taken: walk to the gunner cell and spend 2 AP.
   - **Burst** (4 AP): a cone of cells in a chosen direction inside the sector, up to the effective range. Every target in the cone
     rolls its own hit with a cone-width penalty; everyone in the cone takes suppression.
   - **Sector watch** (all remaining AP, minimum 2): during the opponent's turn the nest fires one burst at the first enemy who
     enters the sector or moves inside it in view. Once per opponent turn.
   - **Belt change** 3 AP. Turning inside the sector is free in turn-based; the sector itself is fixed by the nest.
8. **Ownership** = the gunner's side. Gunner dead or gone -> the nest is neutral. No separate capture: whoever sits fires.
9. **AI gunner** = an occupied post (T-11 sense). Fires at visible enemies in the sector; in turn-based sets sector watch when it sees
   nobody on its turn. Dismounts and falls back to the nearest cover when an attacker is outside the sector closer than 8 m, or when
   its suppression reaches the threshold.
10. **Player control (real time).** Order "Man the gun" on a nest; the barrel follows the cursor inside the sector at the traverse
    limit; fire / attack-target as usual; separate order "Leave".
11. **Level generator** places a nest only when a flank route exists: a route to a cell outside the sector, at most 40 m long, out
    of the gun's line of fire. No route -> no nest. The smoke checks the same.
12. **Visuals.** The designer's animations (mount, fire, belt change, dismount, death at the gun). Until a model exists: placeholder
    mount + barrel + sandbags, like the mortar nest.

## Tuning Knobs

All values live in `Content/Data/Weapons/weapons_tuning.json`, block `mg_nest` - not hardcoded.

| Knob | Default | Range | Category | Rationale |
|------|---------|-------|----------|-----------|
| sector_deg | 110 | 90-120 | gate | covers a street, leaves the flanks |
| traverse_deg_per_s | 45 | 30-60 | feel | flanking really works |
| cover_arc_margin_deg | 15 | 0-30 | gate | the shield protects only the front |
| rounds_per_min / burst | 600 / 8-12 | 450-800 / 5-15 | feel | fire density |
| damage_mult_vs_rifle | 1.2 | 1.0-1.5 | curve | stronger than the rifle, not a wall |
| effective / max range m | 45 / 70 | 30-60 / 50-90 | gate | crossroads and street |
| penetration_class | heavy | - | gate | high penetration |
| belt / change s | 100 / 8 | 50-150 / 5-12 | feel | silent window |
| overheat_silence_s | 4 | 2-6 | feel | second silent window |
| mount / dismount s | 1.5 / 1 | - | feel | a flanker gets time |
| AP mount / burst / belt / dismount | 2 / 4 / 3 / 1 | - | curve | turn-based |
| watch_min_ap | 2 | 1-4 | curve | watch instead of a burst |
| cone_hit_penalty | -15 % | 0-30 | curve | a burst hits many, but worse |
| ai_dismount_flank_m | 8 | 4-15 | gate | a flanker drives the gunner off |
| levelgen_flank_route_max_m | 40 | 20-60 | gate | the solo player can always go around |

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Rule tests: sector, traverse, cover arc, AP costs, belt and overheat (`CodexTactics.Combat.MgNest.*`).
- [ ] Real-time smoke `MgNestSmoke`: the gunner hits a target in the sector and does not fire outside it; a flank attacker reaches
      and kills the gunner; no fire during the belt change / overheat.
- [ ] Turn-based smoke: a cone burst hits more than one target; watch fires once per opponent turn; a free nest is taken for 2 AP.
- [ ] A player operative takes the nest after the enemy gunner died and fires at the PMC.
- [ ] The level generator refuses a nest without a flank route (a test for the refusal).
- [ ] Feel (designer playtest): at a crossroads with a nest, a solo operative breaks through by flanking or in the silent windows;
      head-on, the gun kills him.
- [ ] No regression: mortar nests, suppression and the PMC smokes unchanged.
