# Kit pilot 2026-11-20: one generated block in the Soviet building kit

Designer look test: building `B_x` of `Content/Data/LevelGen/accepted/gen_bgen_plans_check.json` (the L_Gen_BgenPlans block,
2 storeys, 12 x 36 m, two sections) dressed with the modules of the Soviet building pack (`Content/SovietHouses`, untracked, never
committed) on its own map `/Game/Maps/KitPilot/L_KitPilot` (not committed: it references the pack). The generator and
`build_levelgen_map.py` are unchanged; the other 8 buildings are left out.

Script: `Scripts/Editor/kit_pilot_building.py` (the docstring has the exact command lines).

1. `build` (editor commandlet, `-nullrhi`): plans the layout with the builder's own `plan()` (the builder is loaded as a library, its
   `main()` is not run), keeps the building's grey boxes as **hidden proxies** and places the kit visuals over them; writes
   `Content/Data/LevelJson/L_KitPilot.json` (points at the original accepted layout; environment locked Clear 13:00, no snow cover) and
   `Saved/KitPilot/kit_pilot_report.json` (counts, every stretch factor, every squished filler).
2. `Scripts/bake_navmesh.ps1 -Map /Game/Maps/KitPilot/L_KitPilot`.
3. `shots` (in game, `-game -RenderOffscreen`, `py <copy without spaces>`): the map's CameraActor (tag `KitPilotCam`) is moved through
   the views, `HighResShot 1920x1080`, pictures copied to `Saved/KitPilot/shots/`; first it checks in game that the hidden proxies still
   block sight and the navmesh and that the openings are still open.

## How the proxies work

| Piece | In the pilot |
| --- | --- |
| Facade walls, sills, lintels, door leaves, partitions, parapets, stair ramps (the generator's cubes) | kept, **hidden** (hidden in game + component invisible), collision / navmesh / tags unchanged |
| Window glass panes (visual only in the generator) | dropped: the kit window has glass |
| Floor / roof slabs | kept visible (grid); kit floor finish planes 0.5 cm above |
| Kit modules | visual only: `NoCollision`, `CanEverAffectNavigation` false |

So cover, sight lines, mantle edges, door states and the walking ramps are the generator's, bit for bit. The kit visual of an opening
differs from the real cut-out by a few cm (kit window hole 150 x 91..241 cm after the stretch vs the layout's 150 x 95..245).

## Stretch (from kit_pilot_report.json)

Inside the ~10 % rule: every window / balcony / entrance / stair glass bay, windows, doors (x 1.1 / z 1.1), stairs, parapets, porch,
canopy, all facade heights (x 1.0714).

Beyond it (the layout's sizes leave no choice):

- **Facade fillers, 20 of 52.** The layout's windows sit on a 50 cm grid round the rooms (200 cm from a corner, 400-550 cm apart), not on
  the kit's 300 cm bays. A filler slides up to 72 cm behind a window's jambs (300 cm module, 0.3 cm behind) when that covers the gap;
  otherwise it is squished: long-side corner strips 50-65 cm -> x 0.46-0.47 (8), end-wall corners 135 cm -> x 0.79 (8), 55-100 cm
  between bays -> x 0.53-0.82 (4). The Facade paint has no pattern, so the squish is not visible - only the panel joints come closer; `KIT_PILOT_FILLER=squish`
  (contiguous fillers, no overlap) looks the same (`kitpilot_squish_*.png`).
- **Interior walls, 112 of 132 pieces**: short partition segments, the 19 cm jambs beside the 112 cm flat doors (x 0.06), lintels
  (z 0.2-0.25). Plain wall paint, not visible in the pictures.
- **Stairwell walls** z 1.154 (260 -> 300 cm); the 54 cm strip over an entrance z 0.383.

## Problems seen

1. **Stairs do not match the walk line.** The generator's core has full-storey flights (300 cm rise over 429 cm, one lane per storey,
   arrival at the far end); the kit stair is a half-landing switchback (two 140 cm flights, arrival at the same end). The kit set fits
   the core's footprint (325 x 880 cm inside, kit 308 x 560 cm), but an operative on the hidden ramp floats above the kit half-landing or
   cuts through it. Fix belongs in the generator (a switchback core with a half landing: two ramps + a landing per storey) - not done
   here.
2. **Door widths.** The layout's doors are 150 cm (portals 200); the kit's are 80 (room leaf), 102 (flat door), 130 (entrance). Flat doors
   get 19 cm wall jambs, room doors two leaves, the entrance x 1.1 (143 cm visible in a 150 cm opening).
3. **Window grid.** See the fillers above; with window centres at 150 + 300 k from the corner the facade would be kit modules only.
4. **Fake openings.** The stair glass bays and the balcony doors are visual only (the proxy wall behind is solid: they block sight and
   are not reachable). The balconies have no collision.
5. **Roof cutaway** does not hide kit visuals (they have no collision for its trace); the pictures use their own camera.
6. **Snow cover** also lands on interior floors (first pictures); the pilot turns it off.
7. Floor / roof slabs stay grid underneath (ceilings); floor planes use UV 0..1 per 200 cm cell.
8. Court side is in shadow at 13:00 (the generated maps' sun comes from the street side); the stairwell interior picture is dark (no
   light in the core); the view from above is the readable one.
9. Visible seams: the Facade panel joints at every module edge, and a hair line where a filler sits behind a jamb. No z-fighting seen
   (fillers 0.3 / 0.15 cm back, floor planes 0.5 cm up, stair sets 0.6 cm up).
