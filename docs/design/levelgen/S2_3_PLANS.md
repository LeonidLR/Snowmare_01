> the generator emits them, 50 cm per character, long side across, **the street at the bottom** (the court side with the stair core on top). Review before S2.4.

Legend: `#` outer wall, `+ - |` partition, `E` outer door (street / court), `w` window, `S` window that is a balcony / fire-escape slot, `U` flat front door
(**closed** by default), `d` room door, `e` enfilade door, `P` stair-pad portal (200, no leaf), `b` balcony door (only after story 4). Rooms: `K` stair core,
`L` landing / corridor, `O` lobby (ground storey: a room with an outer door), a lower-case letter = a flat with a second entry (a loop), an upper-case letter = a **dead-end** flat (one entry),
`r` room, `s` service room, `e` end suite. Same letter = same flat (a flat is 2-3 rooms: back room, front room, and the core bay's front room beside it).

## How a block is built (what to read in the pictures)

- **Sections.** A block is one or more sections side by side; a section is `flat - core - flat` (a short one, 13-16 m, `core - flat`). 17-23 m long = one section, 34-46 m = two
  (each with its own stair core, landing and entrances; a wall without a door between them - the sections meet on the roof). 24-33 m has no section count: it falls back to the old
  BSP (see B4).
- **The landing** (`L`, 2.5 m wide beside a 2-lane core, up to 4 m beside a 1-lane core) runs the core bay's whole length; the two stair pads open onto it (`P`).
- **A flat** = its back room (beside the core, 4-7 m wide) + its front room (street side) + the front room of the core bay on its side: 3 rooms (2 on the ground storey, where one room per
  section is a lobby). Its front door (`U`, closed) is on the landing's end wall. **One flat per section also has a second entry** (landing - its core-bay front room): that is the
  section's loop (landing - back room - front room - core-bay room - landing). The other flat has one entry = a **dead-end flat** (story 17; the run from the stair pad is in the text).
- **Ground storey.** The street door opens into the core-bay front room of the loop flat (a lobby with a door onto the landing), the court door into the back room of the other flat (also
  a lobby). Each section has both, so each section is an entrance of its own.
- **Slots.** In the front room of every flat, on every storey from 2, the window nearest the room's middle is a balcony / fire-escape slot (`S`). Nothing is built on it: **no balcony without
  an escape** (your answer of 2026-11-19). Story 4 turns the slot window of one flat column into a balcony door on every upper storey and puts the ladder outside (the last picture).
