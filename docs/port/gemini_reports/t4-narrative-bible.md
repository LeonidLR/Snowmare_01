2. **Thematic Pillars & Tone (Homage to *Gorky 17*)**:
   - Dry, tense, bleak, cynical, claustrophobic military realism.
   - Contrast between the suffocating tropical rain/heat of the past betrayal and the dead, freezing desolation of the arctic present.
   - Sub-zero cold treated as an omnipresent active adversary that freezes limbs, impairs marksmanship, and forces thermal triage.
   - Fragile, aging veterans (late 40s to early 50s) with chronic injuries and PTSD facing corporate cutting-edge military hardware.

   - **Marat Babaev ("Babai")**: Heavy Weapons Specialist / Combat Engineer. Third-degree burn contractures from burning APC, quiet brooding rage, deep bass voice, manages heavy barricades, repairs generators, and outputs suppressive fire.
   - **Sergey Roninov ("Ronin") — The Antagonist**: Former comrade turned corporate warlord. Sociopathic pragmatist who traded an entire village and his unit for conflict diamonds to build PMC "Ronin".
   - **Supporting Cast**: The ГБР state handler on radio, the Summer Camp Instructor, and Ivan Susanin.
