## Summary of Implementation

1. **Fast Gate Nav Validation (`Tools/TrainingGround/src/levelgen/props/fastGate.ts`):**
   - Implemented `runFastGate(layout, file, balance, targetLayerId)`.
   - Constructs `NavGrid` per walk layer with nav-affecting props (crates, barrels, turrets) converted to obstacle segments widened by `AGENT_RADIUS = 35` cm:
     - Cylinders (barrels): point segment with `thickness = 2 * radius` (`radius + 35` cm widened radius).
     - Rectangles/Boxes (crates, turrets): 4 rotated perimeter segments + 2 cross-diagonal segments to fully block interior cells.
     - Non-nav-affecting props (mines, loot) are skipped (`nav_affecting: false`).
   - Validates:
     1. Mandatory route & variants completability: every leg on its layer must have an unobstructed path.
     2. Doorways & compound gates reachability: canonical doorways from `PropsLayoutView` checked for reachability from route/spawn.
     3. Ladder foot & top reachability: verified on their respective from/to layers.
   - Fault isolation (`findBlockingProps`): isolates failing leg name (`leg 3`, `doorway B_shed#0`, etc.) and the specific blocking prop object IDs (`gate_blockers`).

2. **Placement Integration (`Tools/TrainingGround/src/levelgen/props/placement.ts`):**

     - When `layout` is omitted (golden test harness), leaves `gate: "n/a"`, preserving 100% byte-for-byte golden fixture parity.
   - Updated `PropsPlacementReport` and `reportToJson` to support optional `gate_leg` and `gate_blockers`.
