## 1. Tactical Concept & Operational Goal

Up to this milestone, `L_Gen_Depot_1` has functioned predominantly as a **Combat Lab** fixture: squad immortality was enabled, ammunition was infinite, and environmental cold acted as a cosmetic HUD vignette.

**Task #46** transforms Depot into a **hardcore tactical survival mission** matching the core vision of *CodexTactics*:
1. **Lethal Stakes (Cheats Disabled):** Squad immortality and infinite ammo are strictly disabled. Hits deal permanent damage, bleedings require hemostatic gauze, and operative death is permanent for the mission run.
2. **Ammunition Depletion & Scavenging Economy:** Weapon firing exhausts magazines; reloads consume reserve inventory. Running out of ammunition forces players to scavenge PMC corpses, breach supply crates, or fall back to sidearms.
3. **Severe Cold & Blizzard Survival:** Exposure to sub-zero winds rapidly drains body heat. Hypothermia impairs aiming stability, induces shivering, and eventually causes lethal freezing tick damage unless warmed by barrels, shelter interiors, or the BTR-82 cabin heater.
4. **Procedural Hazards (Post-Props C4 Integration):** Mines, tripwires, and loot crates generated by Props C4 become active physical hazards and resources within the level geometry.
5. **Mission Lifecycle & Extraction Gate:** Clear objective flow (Infiltration $\to$ Intel/Depot Recon $\to$ Sector Defense $\to$ Exfiltration at Depot Gate), gated by `UMissionSubsystem`.

## 3. Detailed Subsystem Specifications

### 3.1. Subsystem A: Lethality & Medical Recovery
- **Configuration in `L_Gen_Depot_1.json`:**
  ```json
  "cheats": {
    "immortal": false,
    "infinite_ammo": false
  }
  ```
- **Medical Treatment Sequence:**
  - Health $< 0$: Operative enters Incapacitated / Bleeding Out state ($45\text{ s}$ bleedout timer).
  - Medic operative interacts (`E` key, uses Medkit from inventory): restores operative to $25\text{ HP}$ (Wounded tier with limping locomotion per `005-injured-scout`).
  - Squad Wipe: If all living operatives become incapacitated, `UMissionSubsystem::FailMission` triggers the Mission Failure HUD screen.

### 3.2. Subsystem B: Ammunition Scarcity & Management
- **Initial Loadouts:**
  - *Commander (M4A1 / AK-74):* 30 in chamber/mag + 90 in reserve (3 spare mags).
  - *Engineer (Shotgun):* 8 in tube + 24 in reserve (3 full reloads).
  - *Medic (MP5 / Vector):* 30 in mag + 60 in reserve (2 spare mags).
- **Ammunition Caches:**
  - Wooden ammo crates placed via Props C4 contain green ammo canisters (restores 60 rifle rounds or 16 shotgun shells).

### 3.3. Subsystem C: Cold & Environmental Exposure
- **Dynamic Heat Balance:**
  $$\frac{d(\text{Warmth})}{dt} = 
  \begin{cases}
    +4.0 \% / \text{s} & \text{inside heat zone (Barrel, Running APC, Stove)} \\
    -0.8 \% / \text{s} & \text{open outdoors (Calm weather)} \\
    -2.2 \% / \text{s} & \text{open outdoors (Blizzard active)}
  \end{cases}$$
- **Frostbite Tiers:**
  - $100\% - 60\%$: Comfortable (normal aiming).
  - $59\% - 30\%$: Shivering (Weapon spread angle $+35\%$, slight camera jitter).
  - $29\% - 1\%$: Severe Frostbite (Sprint disabled, weapon spread $+70\%$, movement speed $-25\%$).
  - $0\%$: Freezing (Loses $3\text{ HP} / \text{s}$ until warmth restored).

### 3.4. Subsystem D: Active Mines & Defusal Mechanics
- **Actors:** `ABP_TripwireMine`, `ABP_PressureMine`.
- **Detection & Warning:**
  - Approaching within $6\text{ m}$ in Walking or Crouching stance reveals a faint wire glint (custom post-process highlight) and an auditory warning click.
  - Sprinting past or stepping into the trigger zone ($80\text{ cm}$ radius) detonates instantly ($180\text{ damage}$ + knockdown).
- **Defusal:**
  - Engineer approaches in crouch, right-clicks mine $\to$ selects "Defuse".
  - Duration: $3.5\text{ s}$. Successful defusal converts the mine into an inventory explosive charge.

## 5. Failure Modes & Edge Cases

1. **Unavoidable Mine Chokepoint (Softlock):**
   - *Risk:* Procedural generation places a mine across a 1-meter wide corridor with no alternate route and no Engineer in squad.
   - *Mitigation:* Props C4 rule enforces `mandatory-route forbid`: no mine can block a primary critical navigation path without a minimum $120\text{ cm}$ safe walking clearance or shootable detonator barrel nearby.
2. **Squad Freeze Softlock:**
   - *Risk:* Player runs out of heat packs during blizzard and cannot reach next barrel.
   - *Mitigation:* Heat barrel placement guarantees maximum travel distance between thermal zones $\le 45\text{ seconds}$ at normal walking speed.
3. **Empty Ammo Stalemate:**
   - *Risk:* Squad exhausts all ammo against bullet-sponge enemies.
   - *Mitigation:* Every eliminated PMC soldier is guaranteed to drop at least 1 usable magazine for their respective weapon type via `UCorpseSubsystem`.
